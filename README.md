# Safe AI Builders Nigeria

A practitioner AI safety community for the people who actually build and ship AI in Nigeria: startup founders and tech workers. Based in Abuja, open to builders across Nigeria online.

This document is the full plan behind a 6-month founding pilot. It expands on a shorter application submitted to BlueDot Impact's Rapid Grants programme.

---

## Why this exists

AI is being adopted fast across Nigeria, but the people building and deploying it here have almost no organised space to think about safe and responsible use. Risk does not only come from frontier labs. It also comes from how AI gets built and shipped at the edges, often with weak data protection and little safety awareness.

Nigeria already has valuable AI safety efforts, and they skew toward research, policy, and students:

- **AI Safety Nigeria (AISN)** — an Abuja nonprofit running reading groups, a Learn & Launch training series, fellowships, and an annual summit.
- **University student groups** — at the University of Nigeria (Nsukka), Lagos State University, and Ahmadu Bello University (Zaria).
- **Nigeria AI Collective** — a government-initiated community of practice led by Data Science Nigeria, Lagos Business School, and CJID.

What is missing is a space for the **builders** — the founders and engineers making day-to-day decisions about how AI touches real users and real data. Safe AI Builders Nigeria fills that gap, and does it **alongside** these groups, not in competition with them.

---

## Who it is for

- Startup founders building AI-enabled products.
- Engineers, data people, and product managers shipping AI features.
- Technical operators in companies adopting AI.

The common thread: people who make real decisions about AI in production, who need practical safety judgement, not just theory.

---

## How it complements existing work

We will not duplicate what already exists. Early in the pilot we will reach out to **AI Safety Nigeria** and local hubs to:

- co-run or cross-promote events,
- share and point people to existing curriculum,
- route people to the right group for their level (students and researchers to AISN and the university groups; builders to us).

The aim is one healthier ecosystem, with a part that finally speaks to practitioners.

## Home base: Ventures Park

The community has a ready home. We are part of **[Ventures Park](https://www.theventurespark.com/)**, an Abuja workspace and community where our company holds a space and runs its meetings. It has a large community of tech professionals, which gives us:

- a credible, consistent venue for in-person meetups, and
- a built-in audience of exactly the builders we want to reach.

In-person meetups start at Ventures Park in Abuja. The **online hub and the study track are open to builders anywhere in Nigeria from day one**, so the community serves the country, not just the capital.

## What we will do (6 months)

### 1. Founding launch forum
A larger half-day event in Abuja (target 60 to 80 people) to introduce the community, set the tone, and recruit a core group of founders and technical people. Format: a few short talks plus structured discussion.

### 2. Monthly meetups (x5)
In-person at Ventures Park, roughly 25 to 30 people each, every month. Each meetup takes one concrete, builder-relevant theme. Example themes (to be refined with the community):

- **Data exposure:** what are we actually sending to AI services, and where does that data go?
- **Hallucination and verification:** shipping AI features users can trust.
- **Guardrails in practice:** human-in-the-loop, grounding outputs to source, confidence flags.
- **Governance basics for founders:** consent, data handling, and emerging local rules.
- **Evaluating AI features:** how to test an AI feature before it ships.

### 3. Online hub (national)
A community platform (Discord or Circle) plus a simple site, open to builders across Nigeria, so discussion continues between meetups and people outside Abuja can take part.

### 4. Curated study track
A short, guided reading-and-discussion path drawn from open AI safety and governance material, including BlueDot's public curriculum. Delivered as a small cohort, online, so anyone in Nigeria can join. Illustrative modules:

- What AI safety and governance actually mean for a builder.
- Where AI systems fail in production, and how to catch it.
- Practical guardrails and review processes.
- Data, privacy, and responsible adoption.

### The safety insight behind this

While building DeepThread, I ran into a real safety problem first-hand: the system could produce confident meeting summaries that no one in the room could verify, sometimes misattributing points or inventing action items nobody had agreed to. We responded by keeping a human in the loop before anything was sent, grounding summaries back to the transcript, limiting what the AI was allowed to assert, and flagging low-confidence parts. That is exactly the kind of practical safety judgement this community is meant to build.
